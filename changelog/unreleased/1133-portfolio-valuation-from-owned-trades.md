Portfolio valuation, the value, capital and benchmark charts, per-trade valuations on Transactions, unrealized gain/loss, the investment paycheck estimate and the MCP investment portfolio tool now count all of your trades pooled per instrument instead of per account, so trades not linked to an account are valued too: a listing's cost basis is one weighted average across former accounts, bond gain/loss accrues from the latest pooled entry, until the investment account page is replaced its chart, holdings, benchmark and capital cover all of your trades, and the MCP `get_investment_portfolio` tool returns one position per listing with its value instead of per-account values. #1133
