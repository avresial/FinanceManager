Net worth, assets, liabilities, balances and the dashboard now pool your transactions by currency, bond and investment holding instead of by account: every cash balance now shows as Cash (no more separate Crypto, Real estate, Other or Loan classes), a loan nets against cash in the same currency so only a negative net balance counts as a liability, and each bond accrues interest from its latest entry. Cash and bond amounts in a currency other than your display currency are still added as they are, so the cards now say "Includes EUR, not converted", show each holding in its own currency, and the Assets "Breakdown" toggle is now "Holdings" with rows labelled Stock, Cash or Bond that open the matching transactions. #1132
